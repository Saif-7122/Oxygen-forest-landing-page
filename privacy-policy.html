Privacy policy.

Last updated: 1 August 2026

Who we are

This website markets Oxygen Forest (Pranavayu Vanam), a gated community of forest estates at Kamareddy, Telangana. It is operated by House of Investors, a real estate wealth house based in Hyderabad and the authorised mandate partner for the project. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" refer to House of Investors.

What we collect

  • Details you submit in an enquiry form: your name, phone or WhatsApp number, and your answers about purpose, visit preference, and buyer type.
  • Basic visit and campaign information: the page you landed on, referrer, and advertising identifiers such as campaign tags and click IDs, used to measure our advertising.

We do not collect email addresses on this website and we do not ask for financial information here.

How we use it

  • To send you the price sheet, master plan, and estate availability you requested, primarily on WhatsApp.
  • To schedule site visits and video walkthroughs.
  • To measure which of our advertising campaigns lead to genuine enquiries.

How we share it

Your enquiry is shared with the Oxygen Forest sales desk and the project's developer so your request can be actioned. We do not sell your data and we do not share it with unrelated third parties for their marketing.

Retention and your choices

We retain enquiry records for as long as needed to serve your request and meet legal obligations. You can ask us at any time to correct or delete your details, or to stop contacting you, using the contact below.
